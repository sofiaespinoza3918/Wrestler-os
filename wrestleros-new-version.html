Rankings you can actually trust. FloWrestling rankings are based on editorial opinions from a small team. WrestlerOS builds rankings from math — every single match a wrestler has competed in, weighted by how they won. You can see exactly why someone is ranked #1.
→
Recruiting intelligence in one place. Right now you're tracking recruits on spreadsheets, DMs, and 5 different tabs. WrestlerOS puts every available athlete — transfer portal and high school — in one searchable database that filters by weight, GPA, academic fit, and more.

How the Ranking System Works
1

Every Match Counts

Unlike other rankings that only look at big tournaments, WrestlerOS counts every documented match from September through March — dual meets, invitationals, conference championships, and nationals. A wrestler who goes 30-2 against top competition all season will rank higher than someone who shows up hot for one tournament.

2

Wins Are Weighted by How Dominant They Were

Not all wins are equal. Pinning someone earns more credit than barely winning a 4-3 decision. A tech fall shows dominance. A major decision shows clear control. This means a wrestler who consistently beats opponents convincingly ranks higher than one who squeaks by the same competition.

3

Who You Beat Matters

Beating the #1-ranked wrestler in the country earns far more credit than beating a .500 wrestler. The system automatically accounts for strength of schedule — so coaches recruiting from tough conferences get proper credit, and wrestlers who've avoided top competition can't inflate their ranking.

4

Separate Rankings for Each Style

Folkstyle, freestyle, and greco-roman use different rules, different weight classes, and different techniques. A high school wrestler competing internationally in freestyle gets their own separate ranking — this matters when evaluating international recruits or prep wrestlers who compete year-round in multiple styles.

5

Recruiting Database Stays Clean Automatically

When a wrestler commits (fully, not just verbally) or enters the transfer portal as "unavailable," they're removed from recruiting targets. You'll never waste time chasing a prospect who's already spoken for. High school wrestlers who haven't yet committed stay visible. Graduate transfers with degree mismatches get flagged automatically.
