How WrestlerOS rankings work

FloWrestling rankings are editorial. A small team makes judgment calls. There's no formula you can audit.

WrestlerOS is fully algorithmic. Every ranking has a mathematical reason. Based on ELO — the same system used in chess, NFL analytics (EPA), and esports.
E(A) = 1 / (1 + 10^((ratingB − ratingA) / 400))
R'(A) = R(A) + K × winMultiplier × (result − expected)
Strength of schedule is automatic. Beating a 1800-rated wrestler gives far more ELO than beating a 1500. You can't pad your rating with weak wins — the math handles it.

Data persistence: All user-submitted matches are saved to localStorage so they survive page reloads. Migrate to PostgreSQL to share data across users — the schema is identical to this in-browser model.

Verification system: User submissions need 2 confirmations before they affect ELO. This prevents trolling and builds trust with coaches — they know rankings aren't gameable.
